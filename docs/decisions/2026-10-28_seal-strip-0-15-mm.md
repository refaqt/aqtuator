# 2026-10-28 — The seal strip is 0.15 mm thick

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

The thickness of the seal strip has changed several times. The material decision of 2026-09-22
worked with 0.1 mm. On 2026-09-25 a strip on an old linear axis measured 0.25 mm, and the later
models used that value. A thicker strip is stiffer. It bridges the rollers more, and it bends with
more stress over the same roller.

Niels Bosmans chose 0.15 mm on 2026-10-28.

## Decision

The seal strip of the compact stage is 0.15 mm thick. The material stays as decided on 2026-09-22:
EN 1.4310 (AISI 301), cold rolled to a tensile strength of 2050 MPa, with rounded edges.

The value is in the requirements model, on the part definition `SealingStrip` in
`modules/compact-stage/architecture/compact-stage.sysml`.

## Consequences

- **Bending stress drops.** At the same radius, the stress is 0.6 times the stress of a 0.25 mm
  strip. With the real carriage layout and a pull of 125 N, the strip model gives a tightest radius
  of about 20 mm and a highest stress of about 710 MPa.
- **The fatigue limit at 0.15 mm is extrapolated.** Alleima measure 775 MPa at 0.25 mm and
  630 MPa at 0.50 mm. Their trend gives about 900 MPa at 0.15 mm, but that is outside the measured
  range. The question to Alleima about thin strip still stands.
- **The scraper lips move down 0.1 mm.** They still stop 0.15 mm above the strip, so they are now
  1.2 mm high. The scraper fit check uses 0.15 mm.
- **The top rollers reach 1.15 mm below the top of the strip** (1.25 mm with 0.25 mm). They still
  need to move up.
- **Older results used other thicknesses.** The seal strip designer page still opens at 0.25 mm
  with the old sketch layout of the carriage. Its numbers are not the numbers of the real stage.
