# 2026-10-27 — The compact stage uses the 240 mm RSF tape

**Role(s):** cad, architecture

## Goal

Replace the RSF MC 15 MK tape with a 220 mm measuring length by the same tape with a 240 mm
measuring length (MB-MC15-MK-ML240). The measuring length must cover the full travel of the
carriage, also with both dampers fully pressed. No parts may touch. Niels Bosmans asked for this.

## Short answer

- **The encoder now reads over the full travel.** With both dampers fully pressed, the sensor
  stays 2.0 mm inside the measuring length at each end. Between the two damper contacts it stays
  9.0 mm inside at each end.
- **The air gap is still 0.55 mm**, as RSF asks (0.55 ± 0.25 mm, RSF drawing D1158017-04). The head
  and the tape are on the same line, with no sideways offset and no angle.
- **No new contact between parts.** The closest parts to the encoder are the same as before: the
  bottom carriage 0.55 mm from the tape, the guide blocks 1.55 mm from the tape, and the base
  1.0 mm from the head.
- **The base face under the tape is flat and free** over the full 260 mm of the new tape.
- **The limit switches no longer need to keep the carriage inside the measuring length.**

## Work Done

- **Parts library:** the stoq library now has the 240 mm tape. The tape is 260 mm long: the RSF
  drawing gives the tape length as the measuring length + 20 ± 0.2 mm.
- **Base:** the start of the tape moved 10 mm, from x = 351 to 361 mm. The tape now lies from
  x = 101 to 361 mm, and the measuring length from x = 111.8 to 351.8 mm. It stays centred on the
  path of the sensor between the damper contacts (x = 120.8 to 342.8 mm). The mounting face on the
  side wall is one flat face from x = 10 to 430 mm, with no hole, pocket or slot under the tape.
- **Assembly:** the 240 mm tape replaces the 220 mm tape, with the same fixed joint to the base.
  No other part moved.
- **Bill of materials and architecture:** the tape row and the tape description now name the
  240 mm tape. The measuring length in the read interface is now 240 mm.

## Decisions Made

- The interface versions stay the same. The mounting face, the gap, the sideways offset and the
  frames did not change. Only the length of the tape and the start position changed. A 220 mm
  tape still fits on the new base, and the 240 mm tape on the old one. So this is not a breaking
  change, and the repository rules raise a version only for a breaking change.
