# 2026-10-02 — Comparison table of all linear encoders considered so far

**Role(s):** engineering, purchasing

## Goal

Put every linear encoder we have considered in one table. Each row shows the values that the encoder requirements ENC-01 to ENC-11 ask for. A reader can then see at once which encoder fits the compact stage, and which number is still missing.

The requirements are in [`modules/compact-stage/architecture/linear-encoder.sysml`](../../modules/compact-stage/architecture/linear-encoder.sysml). Every error value is a half band: "5 µm" means "within +/- 5 µm". The limits that say "less than" or "more than" are strict.

## What this means

No encoder in the table meets all eleven requirements with a number that we read in the maker's own data sheet. ENC-11 (the allowed angle of the reading head) cannot pass or fail yet, because the stage angles are still open. It is listed as "not confirmed" in every row.

If we leave ENC-11 aside, these encoders have no failing number and no other missing number that matters. They are the short list to check first:

- RSF MS 14 MK and RSF MS 15 MK (1 Vpp, steel tape).
- Numerik Jena LIKgo, and LIKselect with no frame or with frame B (1 Vpp, steel tape).
- Numerik Jena LAKgo and LAK (absolute, BiSS-C). The data sheet does not tie the 10 m/s top speed to a resolution, so ENC-03 is open.
- AMO LMK 1005 with the high accuracy option (1 Vpp, needs an external electronics box).
- Lamotion COIN with 1 Vpp (the scale width is not given, so ENC-10 is open).
- Renishaw TONiC T1030 with an RTLC20-S tape (value taken from the earlier search).

Numerik Jena LIA, Kit L and the older LIK 21, 22, 23 and 41 parts have no published periodic error (ENC-05), so they are open on that point. They are not on the short list.

**No Heidenhain encoder meets the requirements.** On 6 October 2026 we read the current Heidenhain documents: the 02/2025 catalogue, the product sheets up to 07/2026, and the 04/2026 brochure on two-axis (Dplus) encoders. Every open Heidenhain encoder, incremental or absolute, fails at least one hard requirement on a number in those documents. Three things block them:

- Heidenhain has no BiSS-C output. Its absolute encoders (LIC) send EnDat or a Japanese serial protocol, which our drive cannot read (ENC-04).
- Most heads are 16 to 23 mm wide (ENC-06) and 46 or 50 mm long (ENC-08).
- The heads that are small enough read a glass scale that is too thick or too wide (ENC-09, ENC-10).

The three closest parts are the LIP 6081 (head fits, but the scale is 2.9 mm thick and 15 mm wide), the new LIF 6081 (head fits, but the scale is 1.13 mm thick and only made up to 130 mm long), and the LIDA 489 (thin tape, but the head is 19 × 46 mm and the tape is 13.1 mm wide).

All four makers RSF, Numerik Jena, AMO and Heidenhain belong to one group. They are not independent second sources of each other.

## How to read the table

- A value marked "(calc)" is our own conversion. We scaled an accuracy grade in µm per metre to 200 mm. The maker does not state it for 200 mm.
- "n/c" means not confirmed. The number is not in the maker's own data sheet, or we could not open it.
- For a 1 Vpp encoder, the drive sets the resolution by cutting each signal period into steps. The table shows the signal period. We judged ENC-01 on the finest step that the data sheet allows.
- "Fails" lists only the requirements that fail on a number read in a data sheet. "Not confirmed" lists the requirements with no number.
- The head size is written as width, height and length. Width is across the direction of motion. Height is from the scale to the top of the head. Length is along the direction of motion.
- Rows for RSF, Numerik Jena, AMO, Heidenhain and Lamotion were read from the makers' own data sheets and catalogues on 2 October 2026. They list every open (exposed) model we found, incremental and absolute. Sealed (housed) models are left out.
- The Heidenhain rows were read again on 6 October 2026 from the current Heidenhain documents. The notes column names the document and edition. The LIP 382 row still comes from the 06/2021 catalogue, because the 02/2025 catalogue no longer lists it.
- For Heidenhain LIC and LIDA 400 heads, the head height is now the height of the head body (12 mm and 10 mm). The earlier 7.3 and 7.1 mm were the height of the cable outlet. Both values pass ENC-07.
- Rows under "Earlier candidates" come from the earlier searches in the business repository. They were not read again. Their verdicts are the verdicts of those searches.

## Decisions Made

- Sealed (housed) encoders from the five makers are not listed. You chose open encoders only.
- The earlier candidates stay in the table with their earlier verdict, also the ones that were dropped or ruled out.
- A value that decides a pass or a fail counts only when it comes from the maker's own data sheet. This follows [the mistake of 29 September 2026](../mistakes/2026-09-29_loop-rate-from-a-trade-article.md).
- The Render / JEM DAR row for the COIN head is not repeated. The Lamotion COIN rows cover it.

## Open Questions

- **Heidenhain data is from an older edition.** Closed on 6 October 2026. The Heidenhain rows now come from the 02/2025 catalogue and the current product sheets.
- **Heidenhain sizes that are still missing.** The LIF 6081 head length and the LIP 6081 Dplus scale size are not in the documents we read. Heidenhain gives them in separate mounting drawings (ID 1216748 for the LIF 6000). Neither changes the result, because both parts already fail on another number.
- **Head size of the RSF MS 14 and MS 15.** The purchasing note of 2 October lists 36 × 14.8 × 11.3 mm. The drawing in the MS 14 data sheet shows 13.5 mm across the scale, 14.8 mm high and 36 mm long. The 11.3 mm is a mounting dimension. The table uses the drawing. The change does not alter any pass or fail.
- **Numbers read from drawings.** Head sizes, scale sizes and angles were read from drawing images. Check the ones you rely on against the drawings.
- **Disputed fails in the earlier rows.** Some fails rest on the review of 28 September, not on a data sheet. They are the Fagor head width and the output of Delta, Easson, Hengxiang and Smartwin.
- **Two data sheets disagree** for the Lamotion Invar scales (ALi and RXi) on the line accuracy and the top speed. Both are marked in the rows.
- **Weak links.** For several Chinese makers we found no data sheet link. Those cells say "n/c".
- **The stage angles (ENC-11) are still open.** See the log of 2 October on the encoder requirements.
