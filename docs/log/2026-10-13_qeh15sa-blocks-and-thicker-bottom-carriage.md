# 2026-10-13 — Smaller QEH15SA blocks and a thicker bottom carriage

**Role(s):** cad

## Goal

Fit the bottom carriage of the compact stage to the smaller HIWIN QEH15SA guide blocks, and make
the carriage stiffer.

## Work Done

- **Niels changed the bottom carriage for the QEH15SA guide blocks.** These blocks are smaller than
  the blocks of the earlier design. See
  [Load and life of the HIWIN QE15 guide blocks](2026-10-13_qe15-guide-blocks-load-and-life.md).
- **The blocks now sit in a pocket in the carriage.** Because of this pocket, the carriage could be
  made thicker without raising the stage.
- **The carriage is now 20 mm thick, up from 15 mm.** The outside size is 190 × 180 × 20 mm. The
  volume went up from about 250 000 mm³ to about 407 000 mm³. In aluminium, that is about 1.1 kg
  instead of 0.7 kg.
- **The stored measurements of the carriage are up to date.** The CAD check passes.

## Open Questions

- **This is a first version, and several features are still wrong.** Some holes cut into other
  features, and some walls are too thin. Niels will fix these in the next version.
- 61 sizes in the model are still typed numbers, not linked to the parameter sheet.
