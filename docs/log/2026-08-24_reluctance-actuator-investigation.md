## Decisions Made

- Force of 300 N up to 600 N seems possible.
- Hard to make bidirectional.
- Main problem is that these actuators still act quite a distance from the tool tip, which
  will still result in non-collocated control, but also will require much higher forces due
  to the moment load and cantilever.
