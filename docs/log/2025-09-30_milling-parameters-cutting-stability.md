# 2025-09-30 — Milling parameters cutting stability

**Role(s):** engineering

- Influence of milling parameters on cutting stability:

  - [<u>https://claude.ai/share/572302e0-ddfa-42fe-ba85-a1c51b8af857</u>](https://claude.ai/share/572302e0-ddfa-42fe-ba85-a1c51b8af857)

- The following experiments were video recorded

- Tool: [<u>https://www.fraeser-shop.de/vhm-hpc-fraeser-alu-ohne-fase-6-mm-hp-al3l-dlc-060ha.html</u>](https://www.fraeser-shop.de/vhm-hpc-fraeser-alu-ohne-fase-6-mm-hp-al3l-dlc-060ha.html)

  - D6, fz3, L19, 45°, DLC diamond coated

- Chatter tests, ramping on 150 mm block

  - 0.2 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.025mm /tooth

      - No chatter

    - Feed 0.05 mm/tooth

      - No chatter

  - 0.4 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.025mm /tooth

      - Only chatter at 8 mm Ap and up until 13000 rpm

    - Feed 0.05 mm/tooth

      - Chatter at 8 mm Ap up until 17000 rpm and also a bit later

  - <span class="mark">0.6 mm Ae (up to 6 mm Ap)</span>

    - Feed 0.025mm /tooth

      - Chatter up to 14000 rpm at Ap 6 mm

- Chatter test, S24000, 150 mm block

  - 0.6 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.05 mm/tooth S24000

      - Only little bit of chatter at Ap 8mm

  - 0.8 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.05 mm/tooth S24000

      - High-frequency chatter at Ap 8mm

    - Feed 0.069 mm/tooth (5000 mm/min at 24000 rpm)

      - No audible chatter up until Ap 8mm. Surface finish show a bit of chatter at Ap 8mm though.

  - 1.0 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.069 mm/tooth (5000 mm/min at 24000 rpm)

      - High-frequency (about 5kHz) chatter at Ap 8mm

  - 1.2 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.069 mm/tooth

      - High-frequency (about 5 kHz) chatter at Ap 6mm

  - 1.4 mm Ae

    - Feed 0.069 mm/tooth

      - High-frequency (about 5 kHz) chatter at Ap 6mm

- Chatter test at 24000 rpm, 0.2 mm Ae, 3600 mm/min

  - Ap 10 mm

    - No chatter

  - Ap 15 mm

    - No chatter

  - Ap 19 mm

    - No Chatter

- Chatter test at 24000 rpm, 0.3 mm Ae, 3600 mm/min

  - Ap 10 mm

    - No Chatter

  - Ap 13 mm

    - Slight Chatter (400 Hz??), visible marks on surface

  - Ap 15 mm

    - Chatter

  - Ap 17 mm

    - Chatter

  - Ap 19 mm

    - Chatter
