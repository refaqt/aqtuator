# 2026-02-02 — Stability testing aluminium

**Role(s):** engineering

**Stability testing on 150 aluminium block**

- Increase Ap for same Ae and S.

  - Parameters

    - Ae: 0.3 mm

    - S: 10000, 17000, 24000 rpm

    - f: 0.025 mm/tooth

    - Ap: 0-18 mm

  - Experiments setup

    - Zero X, Y, Z on the left

    - Move to X0,Y-2.7,Z0

    - X150 Z-18 F0.025\*3\*S

      - F1 = 0.025 \* 3 \* 10000 = 750

      - F2 = 0.025 \* 3 \* 17000 = 1275

      - F3 = 0.025 \* 3 \* 24000 = 1800

- Increased Ae until 2 mm. Chatter was not audible, but there are chatter marks.

- Decreased to Ae 1.0 mm.

  - Chatter at the higher frequencies.

- For the x-direction chatter machining tests (with movement in y), chatter was less clear and experiments at Ae 1.0 and 1.5 mm were carried out.
