MK21 to AKD2G Cabling

Which cables and plugs the AQTUATOR CS stage needs to connect the MAXWELL MK21 motor and the RSF AK MC 15 B encoder to a Kollmorgen AKD2G-SPE-6V03S-A1F3-0000-A. It shows what Kollmorgen puts in the box, what Kollmorgen sells separately, and what we must buy or make. Status: 25 November 2026, from the Kollmorgen manuals and guides.

Short answer

  • Kollmorgen has no standard motor cable with an open end. Its AKD2G motor cables end in a Kollmorgen motor plug: M23 SpeedTec or M15 ytec. The 2G Cable Guide offers open ends (flying leads UF or a blunt cut UB) only on feedback cables. A motor cable with an open end would be a special order. We can ask.
  • We do not need a Kollmorgen motor cable. The best route is to ask Saho Robot for the MK21 with an open cable end, cut to length. The four power wires go straight into the Kollmorgen X1 plug, which has spring clamps. No soldering is needed there.
  • The motor temperature sensor must go to the encoder plug. The MK21 PTC wires run inside the motor cable. On the AKD2G the PTC input sits on X23, the encoder connector, pins 8 and 9. So two PTC wires leave the motor cable and are soldered into the X23 plug, next to the encoder wires. Kollmorgen must still confirm that pins 8 and 9 work together with BiSS-C.
  • The encoder needs one small adapter cable that we make. The RSF head ends in a standard 15-pin D-sub plug. The AKD2G X23 socket is a 15-pin high-density D-sub. Kollmorgen sells no cable for a third-party BiSS-C encoder.

Three ways to make the motor cable

Recommended

A. MK21 with an open cable end

  • Ask Saho Robot for the MK21 with flying leads instead of the D-sub 9W4 plug, at the length we need (under 10 m for EMC category C2).
  • U, V, W and PE go into the Kollmorgen X1 plug (AKD2G-CON-X1/X2). Strip, add ferrules, push into the spring clamps.
  • The two PTC wires go to the X23 plug (solder).
  • Fewest parts. But the motor cannot be unplugged at the stage.
If the stage needs a plug

B. M15 ytec plug on the motor, Kollmorgen cable

  • Saho Robot fits a TE Intercontec M15 ytec receptacle on the MK21 (our note of 13 October: 3 + PE + 5 contacts, fits the cable).
  • Kollmorgen standard power cable P1-21-015-C1-00-003000 (PUR, 1.5 mm², AKD2G plug on one end, M15 ytec on the other, 3 m) plugs straight in.
  • This cable has no spare wires for the PTC. The PTC still needs its own two-wire cable to X23.
  • Check that our M15 receptacle has the same pin layout as a Kollmorgen AKM motor (cable guide page 18 onwards).
Ask Kollmorgen

C. Kollmorgen special cable with an open motor end

  • Ask DynamicDrives for a power cable P1-21-015-… with flying leads at the motor end. It is not in the standard list, so it is a special.
  • We then solder or crimp our own motor-side plug, or join it to the MK21 cable.
  • Same PTC problem as B.

Motor plug X1

Figure 2. X1 pin layout from the AKD2G manual page 69. The MK21 standard plug (D-sub 9W4) puts U, V, W, PE on power pins A1 to A4 and the PTC on signal pins 1 and 2 (Saho Robot wiring sheet, 16 December 2025).

Encoder adapter cable for X23

One short cable with a standard 15-pin D-sub socket (female, to take the RSF plug) at one end, and a 15-pin high-density D-sub plug (male) for X23 at the other end. The PTC pair from the motor cable is soldered into the same X23 plug. Use a shielded cable with twisted pairs and metal hoods; connect the shield to both hoods.

Where to see the real parts

The Kollmorgen documents show photos and drawings of the plugs and cables. The page links open the PDF at the right page in most browsers.

  • Kollmorgen accessories guide, page 22: AKD2G connector kits and single plugs, including AKD2G-CON-X1/X2.
  • AKD2G installation manual FS1, page 54: drawing of the drive front with every connector.
  • Same manual, page 69: X1 motor plug pin layout. Page 76: X23 pin layout.
  • Same manual, page 97: motor cable connection with a single cable, and the shield.
  • Kollmorgen 2G Cable Guide: the cable part number scheme on page 34, motor connector photos from page 18.
