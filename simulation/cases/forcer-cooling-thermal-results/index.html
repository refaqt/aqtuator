Water cooling of the compact stage motor: temperatures, thermal growth and the printed cooling block

The MAXWELL MK21 linear motor (the forcer) runs all the time at its rated current of 2.0 A. Water at 20 °C flows through the channel in carriage-bottom. This page shows how hot every part gets, how much the stage grows and tilts from the heat, whether the printed cooling block stays below the heat limit of its material, and which pump gives enough flow for six axes and a spindle. It also shows the model, every input and boundary condition, and the checks behind the numbers.
