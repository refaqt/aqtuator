# short-stroke-actuator-concepts — results

First-order pass/fail for three coupling-actuator concepts in a 50 × 50 × 20 mm
envelope. Regenerated by:

```bash
python3 simulation/cases/short-stroke-actuator-concepts/size_concepts.py
```

Requirements are still informal (stated in the task, not yet in
`architecture/*.sysml`): two-sided, > 200 N, > 0.1 mm travel, > 10 Hz, BOM < 100 EUR,
sensor excluded.

Load stiffness used for plant-resonance checks: **0.71 N/µm**
(100 N / 140 µm at the spindle, [2026-01-27](../../../docs/log/2026-01-27_machine-stiffness-measurement.md)).

## Inertial sanity check

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Steel mass of the whole envelope | 0.39 kg |
| Proof mass for 200 N at 10 Hz, ±50 µm | 1013 kg |
| Proof mass at 100 Hz | 10.1 kg |
| Proof mass at 700 Hz | 0.21 kg |

**Fail as a 10 Hz reaction-mass shaker.** Treat all three concepts as coupling
actuators between two structures.

**Fail as 6-DOF × 200 N** in this box. Stretch is z + rx + ry.

## Concept results

### PM-biased differential reluctance — PASS

| Check | Value | Need |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Force | 204 N | ≥ 200 N |
| Stroke | 0.40 mm | ≥ 0.10 mm |
| Mech. resonance vs machine | 476 Hz | ≥ 30 Hz |
| Envelope height | 17.8 mm | ≤ 20 mm |
| Coil MMF | 140 At | ≥ 96 At |
| BOM | ~55 EUR | ≤ 100 EUR |

Coil ~1.4 Ω, 17 mH, 2.7 W at 1.4 A peak. `V_L` 1.5 V at 10 Hz, 15 V at 100 Hz.

### Flextensional multilayer piezo — PASS

| Check | Value | Need |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Two-sided force (preloaded) | 250 N | ≥ 200 N |
| Stroke at 6×, 60 % hinge efficiency | 0.12 mm | ≥ 0.10 mm |
| Mech. resonance vs machine | 388 Hz | ≥ 30 Hz |
| Envelope | 48 × 18 × 18 mm | ≤ 50 × 50 × 20 mm |
| Stack preload vs blocking | 1500 N vs 3200 N | preload < blocking |
| BOM | ~90 EUR | ≤ 100 EUR |

Catalogue analogue: Cedrat APA40SM (54 µm, 260 N, 15 × 27 × 12 mm) proves force and
envelope; stroke is short of 0.1 mm. Driver cost is the BOM risk.

### Lorentz voice coil + 7:1 flexure lever — PASS (peak duty)

| Check | Value | Need |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Force peak | 250 N | ≥ 200 N |
| Force continuous | 104 N | 200 N **not met** |
| Coil stroke | 0.70 mm | ≪ 20 mm |
| Mech. resonance vs machine | 84 Hz | ≥ 30 Hz |
| Envelope height | 17 mm | ≤ 20 mm |
| BOM | ~70 EUR | ≤ 100 EUR |

Rotary 50 µm eccentric alternative: 10 mNm torque but **600 kg** reflected mass and
**5.5 Hz** resonance — fail bandwidth.

## Verdict

All three 1-DOF coupling concepts are physically plausible in the box. Reluctance is
the only one that holds 200 N without a thermal or high-voltage-driver exception.
