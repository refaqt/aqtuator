# strip-seal-fatigue — results

Life of the sealing strip against roller size, material and layout. Regenerated by:

```bash
python3 simulation/cases/strip-seal-fatigue/strip_seal_fatigue.py
```

All values are for a 0.1 mm strip unless another thickness is named. One cycle is one
crossing of the carriage over one point of the strip.

## Materials

Smallest roller that keeps the strip below its measured fatigue limit.

| Material | Standard | 0.1 mm in stock | Bent one way | Bent both ways |
| --- | --- | :---: | ---: | ---: |
| Alleima 11R51 | EN 1.4310 / AISI 301 | yes | 16.3 mm | **23.8 mm** |
| Alleima Hiflex | UNS S42026 | no, from 0.152 mm | 16.8 mm | 22.7 mm |
| IPCO 1850SM | AISI 1065 carbon | no | 17.9 mm | 24.3 mm |
| Alleima Springflex | EN 1.4462 duplex | no | 21.4 mm | 32.4 mm |
| IPCO 1200SA belt | EN 1.4310, soft temper | no | 26.8 mm | 38.6 mm |

EN 1.4310 wins for a reason worth knowing. Its modulus is the lowest of the group at
185 GPa, and bending stress is proportional to modulus. It also reaches a tensile strength
of 2050 MPa at 0.1 mm, because thin strip work hardens more during rolling. At 0.1 mm the
weak temper cannot be bought at all.

## What one carriage pass does

Four rollers at 20 mm, peak bending stress 920 MPa.

| Cycles per pass | Amplitude | Mean | Equivalent reversed | Life |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1.00 | 920 MPa | 0 MPa | 920 MPa | 443,000 |
| 1.00 | 460 MPa | −460 MPa | 460 MPa | unlimited |
| 1.00 | 460 MPa | +460 MPa | 593 MPa | unlimited |

**One pass is one full reversal.** The two smaller cycles are below the fatigue limit and
cost nothing.

## Life against roller size

Four equal rollers, 0.1 mm strip, EN 1.4310 at Rm 2050.

| Roller | Stress | Share of proof strength | Crossings |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 14 mm | 1312 MPa | 0.66 | 19,000 |
| 16 mm | 1149 MPa | 0.58 | 61,000 |
| 18 mm | 1022 MPa | 0.52 | 180,000 |
| 20 mm | 920 MPa | 0.47 | 443,000 |
| 22 mm | 837 MPa | 0.42 | 1,000,000 |
| **24 mm** | **768 MPa** | **0.39** | **unlimited** |

Permanent set is never the limit at this temper. Even 14 mm reaches only two thirds of the
proof strength.

## Opening the two rollers above the carriage

| Rollers 1 and 4 | Rollers 2 and 3 | Crossings | Gain |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 14 mm | 14 mm | 19,000 | — |
| 14 mm | 60 mm | 83,000 | 4× |
| 20 mm | 20 mm | 443,000 | — |
| 20 mm | 40 mm | 1,900,000 | 4× |
| **20 mm** | **60 mm** | **unlimited** | — |

Keeping 20 mm in the slot and opening the two top rollers to 60 mm reaches unlimited life
without changing the strip. At 14 mm the entry roller is too tight for 0.1 mm strip and no
layout rescues it.

## Why kilometres of travel do not measure this

A point of the strip is loaded once each time the carriage crosses it. One back and forth
over a stroke `s` is `2s` of travel and gives two crossings, so one crossing costs `s` of
travel:

```
travel [m] = crossings to failure x stroke [m]
```

Travel before failure, 0.1 mm strip, by the stroke the carriage repeats:

| Rollers | 50 mm | 200 mm | 500 mm | 1000 mm |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 14 mm | 1 km | 4 km | 9 km | 19 km |
| 20 mm | 22 km | 89 km | 222 km | 443 km |
| 24 mm | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |

The same strip lasts twenty times further on a long stroke than on a short one. A bare
kilometre figure therefore describes the duty cycle, not the strip.

For scale: Parker rate the stainless band on their OSP-P rodless drive at 8,000 km.

State the life as **crossings of the carriage over any one point of the strip**, or design
below the fatigue limit and state that no limit applies. For a mixed duty cycle, add up
the damage at the busiest position and replace the strip when the sum reaches one. The
controller already knows every move it commands, so it can count crossings per position
without extra hardware.

## Still open

The published fatigue values are measured in dry air. The stage runs SAMNOS AL, a water
based coolant at pH 7.4, applied as minimum quantity lubrication. In a wet environment
many steels lose the fatigue limit entirely, so the unlimited life rows above are not yet
proven for this machine. Two things are needed: the chloride content of the coolant, which
is not on its data sheet, and a bend test with the real strip and the real coolant.
