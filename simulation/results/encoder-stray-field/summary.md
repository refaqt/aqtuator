# encoder-stray-field — results

Field of the X-axis magnet track at the encoder read head, with and without a steel
shield. Regenerated by:

```bash
python3 simulation/cases/encoder-stray-field/stray_field.py
```

The read head is 30 mm out from the edge of the magnet track. "Middle" means at least 60 mm
from a track end. "End" is the worst point near a track end. The magnets are a worst case.
The real magnet sizes are not known.

## The short answer

A steel plate beside the track lowers the field about 2.5 times. That is not enough for a
1 mT limit. Two thin plates with an air gap give about 0.8 mT in the middle of the stroke.
Near the track ends the field stays at about 2 mT whatever plate is used. The read head
must stay away from the track ends, or the head needs its own local shield.

## Values

| Case | 10 mm up: middle | 10 mm up: end | 20 mm up: middle | 20 mm up: end | Peak B in steel |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| No shield | 2.94 | 8.39 | 2.43 | 7.45 | – |
| One plate, ferritic stainless, 2 mm | 1.24 | 3.28 | 1.12 | 3.03 | 2.16 T |
| One plate, ferritic stainless, 3 mm | 1.21 | 3.17 | 1.06 | 2.90 | 1.39 T |
| One plate, low carbon steel, 3 mm | 1.09 | 2.88 | 0.96 | 2.63 | 1.65 T |
| One plate, low carbon steel, 3 mm, 35 mm tall | 1.00 | 2.31 | 0.78 | 1.74 | 1.68 T |
| Two plates, low carbon steel, 2 + 2 mm, 4 mm air gap | 0.78 | 1.91 | 0.74 | 1.83 | 2.81 T |

Field values are in mT. Magnet surface field in the model: 388 mT.

## How to read the last column

The steel in the model never saturates. Ferritic stainless (403, 430) saturates near 1.5 T,
low carbon steel near 2 T. Where the last column is above that, the real plate is full at
its lower edge, and the real field at the head is somewhat higher than shown. This applies
to the 2 mm stainless plate and to the inner plate of the two-plate shield. Make that inner
plate 3 mm.

The peak value sits in the one cell nearest the magnets and changes with the cell size. A
finer mesh gave 1.71 T for the 2 mm stainless plate. Read it as a warning, not as a figure.

## Not in these numbers

- The coil ends of the mover add an estimated 1 to 3 mT at peak current, and less than
  1 mT at continuous current.
- Steel parts near the head, such as the guide block, can pull extra field toward it.
- The real magnet sizes can move every value by about 50 %.
