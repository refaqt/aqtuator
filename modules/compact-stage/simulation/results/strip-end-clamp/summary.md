# strip-end-clamp — results

Version 3: each strip end is held by one insert, pulled by a countersunk screw. The strip is
0.15 mm thick. Regenerated by:

```bash
python modules/compact-stage/simulation/cases/strip-end-clamp/run.py
```

## The short answer

- **The insert fits.** The groove and the tapped hole are milled and drilled from the top and
  stay in solid material. Nothing sticks out past the end of the base. The flange stands 1 mm
  above the strip.
- **The scraper and carriage-top pass over the insert up to the first damper touch.** The scraper
  lips step back 6.5 mm over 14 mm of width around the insert. Carriage-top needs no change for
  normal use.
- **In a crash, with the dampers fully pressed in, carriage-top and the lips hit the insert.** A
  pocket in carriage-top that is 15 mm long instead of 10 mm, at the insert only, clears
  carriage-top. The lips must then move back into that pocket too.
- **There is enough travel.** From a hand-tight strip (10 N) to 125 N, both strip ends together
  must move 0.11 to 0.24 mm. The countersink gives 0.15 mm at each end, so 0.30 mm.
- **The pull is very sensitive to that travel.** Near 125 N, 0.1 mm more travel adds 50 to
  100 N. So the hole positions decide the pull, not the screw.
- **125 N does not keep the strip off carriage-top.** The strip bows 1.96 mm above the bottom
  rollers, and only 0.35 mm of room is there. It presses on carriage-top with about 186 N. A
  ceiling 1.5 to 2 mm higher brings that down to 2 to 16 N.

## Strip model

The solver of the seal strip designer page, with the real carriage layout read from the CAD and
the faces of both carriage halves as walls. Data: [`strip_pull.json`](strip_pull.json).

| Pull | Bow above the bottom rollers, nothing above | Force on carriage-top, ceiling as drawn | Same, ceiling 2 mm higher | Tightest radius | Highest stress |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 N | 8.00 mm | 196 N | 12 N | 30.8 mm | 451 MPa |
| 50 N | 3.62 mm | 187 N | — | 24.2 mm | 575 MPa |
| 125 N | 1.96 mm | 186 N | 2 N | 19.7 mm | 707 MPa |
| 250 N | 1.23 mm | 178 N | 21 N | 16.3 mm | 858 MPa |
| 500 N | 0.78 mm | — | — | 13.2 mm | 1066 MPa |

"—": the contact solver did not settle at this point, or it was not run.

Room above the bottom roller tops: 0.35 mm. This assumes that the bottom rollers stand 1.5 mm
proud of carriage-bottom, like the top rollers stand below carriage-top. No roller is in the CAD
yet.
