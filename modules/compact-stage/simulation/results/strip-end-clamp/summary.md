# strip-end-clamp — results

Fit check of the strip end clamp, version 2, at each end of the base. Everything is reached and
machined from the top. Regenerated by:

```bash
python modules/compact-stage/simulation/cases/strip-end-clamp/run.py
```

## The short answer

- **It fits, with no collision.** The steps and the vertical tapped holes stay in solid base
  material. The strip, the bars, the pins and the screws do not overlap each other or the base.
- **Nothing sticks out past the end of the base, and nothing new stands above its top.** The
  screw heads stay 2 mm under the top of the base.
- **The bar can move 4 mm down and 2 mm up** from its nominal height. That covers the travel that
  sets the pull, and the tolerance on the strip length.
- **The scraper and the carriage pass over the clamp.** At the end of the usable travel the
  scraper lips keep their 0.15 mm to the strip.
- **The top rollers still hit the end wall when the dampers press in fully** (53 mm³ for the two
  rollers). The clamp does not cause this. The rollers sit 1.25 mm below the top of the strip
  everywhere.
