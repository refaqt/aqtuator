# strip-end-clamp — results

Fit check of the strip end clamp at each end of the base. Regenerated by:

```bash
python modules/compact-stage/simulation/cases/strip-end-clamp/run.py
```

## The short answer

- **It fits, with no collision.** The pockets stay inside solid base material. The strip, the
  plates and the screws do not overlap each other or the base.
- **The base stays 440 mm long.** The nearest new surface is 0.05 mm inside the end face.
- **The scraper and the carriage pass over the clamp.** Nothing stands above the top of the strip.
  At the end of the usable travel the scraper lips keep their 0.15 mm to the strip. With the
  dampers fully pressed in, the lips are past the strip end and keep 1.0 mm.
- **The top rollers hit the end wall when the dampers press in fully.** Each roller reaches 1 mm
  into the wall top (90 mm³ for the two). This is not caused by the clamp. The rollers sit 1.25 mm
  below the top of the strip everywhere, as the scraper log already says.
