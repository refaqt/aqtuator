## The short answer

The carriage is still far stiffer and stronger than this load needs. 200 N moves it 0.0044 mm at
most. The highest stress away from the load points is 7.5 MPa. Aluminium 6061-T6 starts to bend
for good at about 240 MPa (the yield strength, verify for the alloy you buy).

| Result | Value | Before the part changed (2026-10-20) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Largest movement | 0.0044 mm | 0.0041 mm |
| Largest movement down | 0.0040 mm | 0.0037 mm |
| Highest stress, more than 3 mm from a load edge | 7.5 MPa | 5.0 MPa |
| Highest stress anywhere (at a load edge, not real) | 21.6 MPa | 17.7 MPa |
| Mesh | 181,867 nodes, 103,174 tetrahedra, 0.5 to 5 mm | 145,591 nodes, 83,604 tetrahedra |

The new part bends about 9 % more. The highest real stress moved. It is now in the sharp inside
corner where a side wing meets the edge of a front guide block seat (x = 191 mm, y = 14 mm,
z = 16.5 mm). A sharp corner in a mesh gives a stress that grows a little as the mesh gets finer,
so read 7.5 MPa as "below 10 MPa". It is still more than 30 times below the yield strength.

The model now holds the four block seats as 35 × 58 mm faces (they were 35 × 48 mm). It holds the
whole face. If the real block touches only part of a seat, the real carriage bends a little more
than the model says.

## What the numbers do not show yet

- **This is not the load that matters most.** The MK21 is an iron core motor. Its forcer pulls
  down towards the magnet track with about 963 N (from the MK2 mover parts list in `modules/stoq`,
  not yet checked against the data sheet). That is about five times this load, and it acts on the
  forcer pocket in the middle, not at the corners. Add it as the next load case.
- **The blocks are rigid in this model.** The QEH15SA blocks and the rails give a little, so the
  real stage moves more than 0.004 mm.

## Requirements

No requirement in [`architecture/compact-stage.sysml`](../../../architecture/compact-stage.sysml) sets a stiffness for the
carriage. Two requirements are close:

- `STG-05` LinearErrorMotion: the carriage stays within 2 µm of a straight line, up and down and
  sideways. A constant load gives a constant bend, which is not an error along the travel. A load
  that changes along the travel, like the magnet pull over the poles, does count.
- `STG-13` LateralLoad: 500 N sideways at the centre of the carriage. That is a different load
  case from this one and is not checked here.
