## The short answer

The carriage is far stiffer and stronger than this load needs. 200 N moves it 0.004 mm at most.
The highest stress away from the load points is 5 MPa. Aluminium 6061-T6 starts to bend for good
at about 240 MPa (the yield strength, verify for the alloy you buy).

| Result | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Largest movement | 0.0041 mm |
| Largest movement down | 0.0037 mm |
| Highest stress, more than 3 mm from a load edge | 5.0 MPa |
| Highest stress anywhere (at a load edge, not real) | 17.7 MPa |
| Mesh | 145,591 nodes, 83,604 tetrahedra, 0.5 to 5 mm |

## What the numbers do not show yet

- **This is not the load that matters most.** The MK21 is an iron core motor. Its forcer pulls
  down towards the magnet track with about 963 N (from the MK2 mover parts list in `modules/stoq`,
  not yet checked against the data sheet). That is about five times this load, and it acts on the
  forcer pocket in the middle, not at the corners. Add it as the next load case.
- **The blocks are rigid in this model.** The QEH15SA blocks and the rails give a little, so the
  real stage moves more than 0.004 mm.

## Requirements

No requirement in [`architecture/compact-stage.sysml`](../../../architecture/compact-stage.sysml) sets a stiffness for the
carriage. Two requirements are close:

- `STG-05` LinearErrorMotion: the carriage stays within 2 µm of a straight line, up and down and
  sideways. A constant load gives a constant bend, which is not an error along the travel. A load
  that changes along the travel, like the magnet pull over the poles, does count.
- `STG-13` LateralLoad: 500 N sideways at the centre of the carriage. That is a different load
  case from this one and is not checked here.
