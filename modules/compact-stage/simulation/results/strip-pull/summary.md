# strip-pull — results

The strip model for the 0.15 mm seal strip in the real carriage. Regenerated by:

```bash
python modules/compact-stage/simulation/cases/strip-pull/run.py
```

## The short answer

- **Little travel is needed for 125 N.** From a hand-tight strip (10 N) to 125 N, both strip ends
  together must move 0.11 to 0.24 mm, depending on the ceiling.
- **The pull is very sensitive to that travel.** Near 125 N, 0.1 mm more travel adds 50 to
  100 N. So the positions of the holes in the strip and in the base decide the pull.
- **125 N does not keep the strip off carriage-top.** The strip would bow 1.96 mm above the
  bottom rollers, and only 0.35 mm of room is there. It presses on carriage-top with about 186 N.
  A ceiling 1.5 to 2 mm higher brings that down to 2 to 16 N.

proud of carriage-bottom, like the top rollers stand below carriage-top. No roller is in the CAD
yet.
