# strip-seal-fit: summary

Rollers 2 and 3 go 0.5 mm down; the seal strip is pulled to 270 N. Then the 0.15 mm strip touches
neither carriage half, and its fatigue life is infinite with 32 % margin on the extrapolated
fatigue limit of 903 MPa. Pulls from 190 to 350 N also meet both goals with 20 % margin. A drop
of 1 mm or more puts the strip on the flat top of carriage-bottom.

The preload screw sits 6.5 mm from the base end, with the magnet ring cut in two around it. The
strip end then carries the 270 N with a factor of 2.7 to yield. At 4.5 mm it would yield.
